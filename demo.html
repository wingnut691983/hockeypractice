Demo Group Walkthrough

Welcome — help us shape StatsLogic

You're one of a small group trying out StatsLogic before it goes wider. This page is a short, guided tour: a Viewer walkthrough, then a Statistician walkthrough, then a few questions we'd love your take on. Should take about 15 minutes.

Share Feedback → Start the tour

1What is StatsLogic?

StatsLogic is a stats tracker built for youth hockey teams. A coach or team statistician enters goals, assists, shots, saves, and a few other stats as the game happens; parents and players see a live, sortable view of those numbers from any device. No spreadsheets, no app installs, no Saturday-morning re-typing.

For the demo, we've set you up with a fictional team so you can poke around without affecting anyone's real data.

2What you'll need

The invite email sent to you contains two short access codes:

  • View code — read-only access. This is what a parent or player would use.
  • Stat code — the elevated tier. This is what a team statistician would use to enter live stats during a game.

Both codes are for the same team (a fictional Demo team under a Demo Association). You'll use each code in turn during this tour.

3Tour as a Viewer (5 minutes)

This is the experience a parent or player has — read-only stats from their phone or laptop.

  1. In the left sidebar, click Browse Associations (or use the link at the top of the page).
  2. Find the Demo Association and click into it. Click the Demo Team on the association page.
  3. You'll land on a sign-in page. Enter your View code in the "Team access code" field and click Continue with code.
  4. You're now on the Team Dashboard. Scan the team's totals at the top, then explore:
    • Click any column header on the Skaters or Goalies table to sort.
    • Click any game row to open the per-game breakdown.
    • Click a player name to open their profile and per-game stat lines.

4Tour as a Statistician (5 minutes)

This is the bench-side experience — the person tapping in stats during a live game.

Best on a tablet. Live Stats Entry is designed for tablets — big jersey buttons and +/− targets sized for thumbs on the bench. It works on a PC or phone too, but if you have a tablet handy, try this section there as if you were actually tracking stats during a game.
  1. Still on the Dashboard? In the left sidebar, click End Viewer code (it'll be in the bottom of the sidebar). This signs you out of the team code session.
  2. Click back into the Demo Team from the sidebar or Browse page.
  3. On the sign-in page, enter your Stat code.
  4. Back on the Dashboard, find a game in the games list and click Live Stats Entry (the green button on each game row).
  5. On the Live Stats Entry screen:
    • Tap a jersey number at the top to bring that player's stat card into focus.
    • Use the + / − buttons to record stats. Every tap saves immediately — no Save button.
    • Switch between Period 1 / 2 / 3 / Final at the top to record per-period stats.

5What we're hoping you'll tell us

Anything is fair game, but if you're stuck for what to write about, here are the questions we care most about:

  • Was it obvious where to click, or did you get lost? Where?
  • Did anything feel slow, broken, or just weird?
  • If you ran a team, would you actually use this? Why or why not?
  • What's the one feature that's missing for you to seriously consider it?
  • Anything that surprised you — good or bad?
